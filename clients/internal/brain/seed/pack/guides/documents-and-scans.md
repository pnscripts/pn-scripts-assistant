# Documents, spreadsheets and scans

Ask me to read, summarise, compare, correct, translate or write a document,
and I work with the file itself.

## What I read
- PDF, Word (.docx), OpenDocument (.odt, .ods, .odp), PowerPoint (.pptx),
  plain text and Markdown.
- Spreadsheets (.xlsx) as rows, each sheet under its name, with the numbers
  where they belong.
- Older formats (.doc, .xls, .ppt, .rtf) when LibreOffice is installed.
- **Scans and photos** — a scanned contract, a photo of a receipt, a PDF from
  a copier with no text in it. The text is recognised on this computer:
  by Tesseract when it is installed (with its Bulgarian and English data), or
  otherwise by a local model that can see. Recognised text can be wrong, so I
  say when text came from a scan; check names, numbers and amounts against the
  original before relying on them. A scanned PDF is read page by page, up to
  thirty pages at a time.

If nothing on the computer can read scans, I say what to install: Tesseract
(on Linux the packages tesseract-ocr and tesseract-ocr-bul) or a model that
can see. With your permission I can install them myself.

## What I write
- PDF, Word (.docx), plain text and Markdown.
- **Spreadsheets (.xlsx)** from a table: the first row becomes a bold header
  that stays in view, columns get a sensible width, and numbers become numbers
  you can add up — 1 200,50 as well as 1200.50. Codes with a leading zero, and
  a number like 1,250 that means different things in different countries, are
  kept as text rather than guessed.

## Changing a document
Corrections go into the existing Word or OpenDocument file without losing its
layout. Anything larger becomes a new version beside the original. The
original is never deleted or replaced unless you ask.
