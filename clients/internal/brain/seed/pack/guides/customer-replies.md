# Customer replies from your own words

Open **Customers** in the menu, or ask: "answer this customer", "reply to
the complaint from Ivan", "what does my answer book say about delivery?".

## Your answer book and your documents
Replies are written only from what your business has written down:

- the **answer book** — the questions customers ask, answered the way you
  want them answered;
- the documents in your **business folder** — price lists, terms, delivery
  and return rules, opening hours — read on this computer (text, Markdown,
  PDF, Word, OpenDocument, spreadsheets).

Set your business name, the tone (friendly or formal), your signature and
the folder under **Your business**. A good reply you have just corrected
can be kept in the answer book with **Save in the answer book**, so the next
customer who asks gets it word for word.

## A reply, checked before you see it
Paste the customer's message, or ask for a reply to a message in your mail.
I tell what kind of message it is — a question, an order, a complaint, a
refund, a cancellation — and write a short reply in the customer's language
from the passages of your own words that match it. Then it is checked:

- a question your words do not answer is not guessed at: it is left in the
  reply as **[[ASK: …]]** for you to fill in;
- a promise of money or time — a refund, a discount, compensation, a free
  item, a delivery date — that your own terms do not already make is marked
  for your decision;
- a price, date or other figure that is not in your words is marked;
- a message about something on your **Always leave to me** list (a lawyer,
  the press, wholesale…) is marked for you.

Below the reply you see what it was written from.

## Nothing is sent without you
**Keep as a mail draft** puts the reply, as you changed it, on the **Mail**
page, where it waits for your yes like every other reply. A draft that still
has an [[ASK: …]] gap in it cannot be sent at all — the customer would see
your note. The scam check looks at the message being answered, as for all
mail. Your answer book and business details are kept in the assistant's
knowledge base on this computer, sealed with everything else.
