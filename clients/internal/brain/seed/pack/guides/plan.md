# Plan: what is next, to-do lists and your calendar

Open **Plan** in the menu, or ask: "what is next?", "what is my week like?",
"put milk on the shopping list", "tick off the tax", "put the parents'
evening in my calendar on Tuesday at six".

## What is next
One list, in time order, of everything coming up: appointments in the diary
and in your online calendar, reminders, and to-dos that are due. Overdue
to-dos come first, in red; to-dos with no date come last. If the online
calendar cannot be reached, the rest is still shown and the page says so.

## To-do lists
Things to do that have no hour of their own. Give each a list — shopping,
home, work, or any name — and, if you like, a day it is due. Tick an item off
with one click and untick it the same way; ticked items stay visible for two
weeks under **Done lately**. In the chat, name an item by a few of its words;
if several match, I ask which.

For something with an hour, use the diary or a reminder instead: the diary
keeps appointments, a reminder speaks up at its moment.

## Your online calendar
Most calendars live on a server — Nextcloud, Fastmail, iCloud, a hosting
company — and all of them speak CalDAV. Give the calendar's own CalDAV
address (in its settings, often called "CalDAV link"), your user name and an
**app password** made for this purpose, and press **Check and save**: the
server is asked before anything is kept, so a wrong address or password is
said at once.

From then on its appointments appear under What is next, and you can ask me
to put a new one in it — I show it to you first. I never change or delete
anything in your online calendar. The address must start with https://, so
the password never travels readable; the password goes only to that address.
**Remove** forgets the address and the password.

## Bringing a calendar file in or out
The diary on the Room page can also read a .ics file and write one out, for a
calendar that is not on a server.
