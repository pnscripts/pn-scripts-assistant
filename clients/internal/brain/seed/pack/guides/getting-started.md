# Getting started

PN Scripts Assistant is an assistant that runs on your computer, remembers what
it learns from your folders and does real work with its tools.

## Everything starts in the chat
Type or say what you want. Short questions get an answer. Bigger requests —
"make me a space strategy game", "write a business plan for a bakery", "sort my
inbox" — become a job: a short plan of steps, each done by the expert whose
work it is, with progress and the result shown in the same chat.

## When it asks
It asks one question at a time, with two to four answers to click. The
recommended answer is first and marked. You can always answer in your own
words instead, by number ("2") or by saying the answer.

## What it never does without your yes
Paying for anything, sending or publishing as you, deleting your files outside
the job's own folder, and changing the system for every user.

## Where things are
- Your brain: one folder (on this computer, or beside the program in a
  portable copy). It holds the memory, the settings and this knowledge folder.
- Knowledge: this folder. Anything put here is learned.
- Playbooks: step-by-step procedures it follows; your own versions win.
