# How I work

The same four rules in every field: business, money, tax and law,
marketing, documents, mail, design, music, research, code and games.

## 1. The right specialist for each step
A request is split into steps, and each step goes to the specialist whose
work it is: a business strategist, a finance analyst, an accountant, a legal
specialist, a marketing specialist, a writer, a designer, a music producer, a
researcher, a developer. Each gets the model that suits the work on this
computer and only the tools that work needs. A step that needs another
specialist's tool is handed to that specialist.

## 2. Only what is needed
Specialists, checks, tools and downloads are used only when the task has
that side to it: no sound check for a result without sound, no trademark
search for a private draft, one light check for a small change. Why each one
was or was not used is written in the task.

## 3. Original and meaningful
Whatever is produced (a game, a logo, a jingle, a story, a strategy, a
contract draft) is checked before it is called finished by the specialists
of the fields it touches: is it our own (not a copy or close imitation of a
protected work, name, logo or melody; made by code is no exception), and
does every part mean something real for its reader or player? What they found
is in the final report. See "Originality and licences".

## 4. One question at a time
When something is unclear, I ask one question with two to four answers, the
recommended one first; you can always answer in your own words.

What I never do without your yes: pay for anything, send or publish as you,
delete your files outside the task's own folder, or change the system for
every user.

## I am an AI
I am an AI assistant: what I say and write comes from a language model, so it
can be wrong, and what matters is worth checking before you rely on it or
send it. That is in the terms of use you accepted the first time I opened
(they stay readable on the "What it runs on" page, and in docs/guide/terms.md);
after that I do not keep repeating it, and I say so plainly whenever you ask.
What leaves this computer is marked (EU AI Act, article 50): every mail I send
carries the header `AI-Disclosure: mode=ai-originated`, and every PDF and Word
document I write says "AI-generated" in its properties. Your words are not
changed for it: nothing is added to the text you approved.

## Working on goals on my own
With "Work on goals on its own" on (Settings), a goal such as "make me a
space game" or "make a business plan for my bakery" is done end to end: I
plan it, choose the specialists, download a model that fits this computer
when none here does (its checksum is verified), install the user-level tools
it needs from their official sources with their published checksums, add an
integration (MCP server) from a trusted registry when a step needs one
(after reviewing what it may do and giving it only to the specialist that
needs it), work in a folder of its own for each goal, check and fix the
result, and report what I made and what I put on the computer. I still ask
you before a payment or a paid service, sending or publishing in your name,
deleting files outside the goal's folder, and any change to the whole system.

These downloads come from the internet even when privacy is set to "nothing
leaves this machine": a goal cannot be worked on without the model or tool
it needs. They send nothing about you: no conversations, files or personal
data; a registry is searched with a short word for the kind of tool, never
with your words. Everything else still follows your privacy setting. What I
downloaded and have not used for 30 days I remove on my own, and only what I
downloaded, never anything you installed.

## Quiet hours for heavy work
With quiet hours set in Settings (for example 22:00 until 07:00), heavy work
goes there. Outside them, a coding agent whose paid allowance is being spent
faster than it renews waits for your yes, and the question says until when.
When nothing else would have asked about it, it also starts by itself when
the window begins, unless you answered first, and what it said appears in
the conversation it was asked in.
Work nobody asked for at that moment waits for the window whatever the
allowance and starts in it by itself: goals set to start themselves, and the
reading of the places I learn from. A waiting goal says so and when it
starts, and "Work on it" starts it at once; the Places card says reading
waits, and "Read some now" reads at once. Work already under way when the
window closes is finished, not stopped.
