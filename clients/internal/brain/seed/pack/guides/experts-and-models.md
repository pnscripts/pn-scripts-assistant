# Experts, models and tools

Every step of a job is done by an expert — a developer, a writer, a
researcher, a designer — and each expert gets the model and the tools its work
needs, chosen at that moment from what this computer has.

## How the model is chosen
- Code work prefers a model made for code; hard thinking prefers a larger
  model; quick replies prefer a fast one; pictures need a model that can see.
- On a computer without a graphics card, waiting time counts, so everyday work
  goes to a smaller, faster model.
- A model that fails twice is tried last until it works again.
- Cloud models are used only for services you switched on, and nothing paid is
  used unless you allow it.
- The choice and the reason are shown with the step.

## Choosing yourself
Give an expert its own model with "prefers" in its profile; that choice always
wins while the model is installed.

## Tools
Each expert can use only the tools its work needs. Anything risky still goes
through the approval rules, whoever asks for it.
