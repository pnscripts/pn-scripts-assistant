# Invoices and VAT — information, not advice

Open **Invoices & VAT** in the menu, or ask: "add this invoice",
"import my sales for September from sales.xlsx", "what is my VAT for
September?", "how near am I to the VAT threshold?".

## What it is, and what it is not
It keeps a register of the invoices you issue and receive, checks them, and
works a month's VAT out from them. It is **information, not tax advice**:
the rules it uses are the law as it read on the date shown beside them, and
the law changes. It never files anything. Check with your accountant or the
tax authority before you file a return or pay.

## The rules, with their sources
For **Bulgaria** the rules come from the VAT Act (ЗДДС) as amended in force
from 1 January 2026, each with its article and the date it was last checked:
the standard rate of 20 % and the reduced rate of 9 % with what it covers,
the zero rate and the basis it needs, registration above €51,130 of yearly
turnover, the monthly tax period with the return and the payment due by the
14th of the next month, invoices issued within 5 days, a ten-digit invoice
number and what an invoice must contain. **The rules used** lists them with
links to the sources. For **another country**, give your own rates and
currency: nothing is then said about that country's law.

## Every invoice checked
As an invoice is added — typed in, read from a PDF, Word file or scan, or
imported from a table — the VAT is worked out when it is missing and checked
when it is given: it must be the rate of the net to the cent, and the total
must be the net and the VAT. The rate must be one of the country's; a zero
rate needs its legal basis; reverse charge shows no VAT; an issued invoice's
number must have the country's form; a VAT number must look like one; the
date must not be in the future. An issued invoice whose number is already
in the register is refused, and numbers missing from the sequence are shown.

## A month's VAT and the threshold
**Work out the VAT** adds up, exactly, the VAT charged on sales and the VAT
on purchases by rate, says what is to pay or to deduct and when it is due,
and checks every invoice of the month again. Below it, the year's sales are
measured against the registration threshold, with the invoice on which they
passed it. The register is kept in the assistant's knowledge base on this
computer, sealed with everything else.
