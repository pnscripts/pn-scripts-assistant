# The scam guard

Every email the assistant reads for you is checked before you act on it, and
the result is written under the message ("Scam check: …").

It looks for: a sender domain that does not exist, names of well-known
companies on someone else's domain, look-alike links, replies that go to a
different address, links hidden behind words like "Verify your account", and
pressure ("suspended", "final notice") combined with a request for a password,
a code, money, bank details, remote access or an install.

A warning means look twice. No warning does not prove a message is genuine.

What to do with a suspicious message: do not click, reply, pay or install
anything from it; check the company on its own website, typed by hand; ask the
assistant "is this a scam?" for a full explanation.
