# Writing and proofreading

I write, edit and proofread in English and Bulgarian. Open **Writing** in the
menu, or just ask in the chat: "proofread this", "make it more formal",
"shorten it to 150 words", "what did you change?".

## Proofreading by rules
Proofreading does not use the language model. It checks what can be proved
from the text itself, so it is instant, it works without a model, and nothing
leaves this computer:

- doubled words, two spaces, a space before a comma or none after it;
- a sentence that does not start with a capital letter;
- a bracket or quotation mark without its partner;
- a Latin letter hidden inside a Cyrillic word (a and а look the same, but
  searches and spell checkers treat the word as a different one);
- Bulgarian: в/във and с/със, the short pronoun ѝ, „не“ written apart from a
  verb, по-/най- with a hyphen, the full article after a preposition, the comma
  before че, защото, но, който, когато, and „…“ quotation marks;
- English: a/an by sound (an hour, a user, an MBA), could of → could have,
  more then → more than, the pronoun I, common misspellings.

Every correction comes with its reason. The sure ones are ticked and can be
made in one press; the others are shown for you to decide, because a rule
that is usually right is still sometimes wrong. Clicking a correction shows
where it is in the text. Meaning, style and facts stay yours to judge.

## Tones and length
Choose a tone — formal, friendly, concise, persuasive, simple or polite — and
proofreading also notes what does not fit it: sentences that are too long,
words that do not belong, contractions and exclamation marks in a formal
letter, words in capitals. Give the most words and it says how far over the
text is.

**Rewrite in this tone** asks the language model for a new version in the
same language, keeping every fact, name, number and link. The new version is
proofread and shown as changes — taken out in red, put in in green — and
replaces your text only if you choose **Use this version**.

## Comparing two versions
Paste two versions under **Compare two versions**, or ask "what changed
between these two files?". The comparison is word by word and is computed,
not described from memory.

## In documents
I can proofread a .txt, .md, .docx, .odt or .pdf file and put the corrections
into a Word or OpenDocument file without losing its layout. The original is
only changed when you say so.
