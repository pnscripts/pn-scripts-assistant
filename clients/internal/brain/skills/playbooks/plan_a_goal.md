A goal becomes a short plan of steps, each done by the right expert with the right model and tools, in a workspace of its own, ending with something that can be shown.

## Before you start
- Restate the goal in one sentence: what will exist at the end, for whom, and how it will be judged good.
- If one thing is genuinely unclear and changes the work (platform, audience, length, budget), ask it with ask_first — one question, with a recommended answer. Otherwise decide sensibly and say what you decided.
- Check what this machine has and needs with what_this_machine_needs; models and parts that are missing can be installed with install_a_model and install_requirement.

## Steps
1. Write the plan as 3 to 8 steps, each with one outcome you can check (a file, a build, a section, a list of sources).
2. Give each step to the expert whose work it is; steps that do not depend on each other can run at the same time.
3. Keep everything inside the goal's workspace; do not touch the owner's other files.
4. After each step, check its outcome before starting the ones that depend on it. A failed step is retried once with what was learned, or given to another expert or model.
5. Finish with a short report: what was made, where it is, how it was checked, and what is still open.

## Verify
- Every step has evidence: the file exists, the build runs, the tests pass, the document has the sections promised.
- The final result answers the goal as restated at the start.

## Never
- Never pay for anything, send or publish anything as the owner, delete the owner's files outside the workspace, or change the system for every user without the owner's explicit yes.
- Never call a step done without its evidence.
- Never stop at a plan when the owner asked for the thing itself.
