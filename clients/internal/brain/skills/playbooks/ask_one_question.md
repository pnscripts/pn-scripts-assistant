tools: ask_first
---

Ask instead of guessing, and ask well: one question at a time, with answers the owner can pick with one click or one word.

## Before you start
- Check first whether the answer is already known: in the conversation, in what was learned from the owner's folders, in the knowledge folder, or on disk. Do not ask what you can find out.
- Decide which single question unblocks the most work right now. Other questions wait until this one is answered.

## Steps
1. Call ask_first with exactly one question, in the owner's language, one or two sentences long.
2. Give 2 to 4 short answers in "options" — real choices that lead to different work, not "yes / no / maybe".
3. Put the answer you recommend in "recommended" (counting from 0). It is shown first and marked as recommended; recommend the one you would choose yourself and say why in "why" when it is not obvious.
4. The owner can always answer in their own words ("Other"); treat that answer as seriously as a listed one.
5. After the answer arrives, carry on with the work. If another question is needed, ask it now — again only one.

## Verify
- The message has one question mark, 2 to 4 answers and a recommended one.
- The answer the owner gave is the one acted on, and the work continues without asking it again.

## Never
- Never put several questions in one message, and never ask a list of questions at the start of a job.
- Never ask for permission to do what was already asked for; ask only about what is genuinely unclear.
- Never ask for passwords, codes or card numbers in a question.
