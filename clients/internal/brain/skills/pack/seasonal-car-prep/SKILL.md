# Seasonal car preparation

Most seasonal breakdowns are predictable: weak batteries in the cold, overheating and tyre failures in the heat. A short check before the season prevents them.

## Steps
1. **Collect the facts.** Car type and age, fuel or electric, current tyres (type and age), climate and the roads usually driven (mountains, motorway, city), planned trips, and country or countries of travel.
2. **Check legal requirements.** Many countries require winter tyres or chains in certain periods or conditions, and some require equipment (warning triangle, first-aid kit, high-visibility vest, fire extinguisher). Tell the user to confirm the current rules on official sources for each country they will drive in.
3. **Run the season checklist** below, marking each item ok / do it / garage.
4. **Prepare an emergency kit** suited to the season.
5. **Schedule garage work** early in the season, before demand peaks.

## Winter checklist
- Tyres: winter or all-season tyres with adequate tread (many experts advise at least about 4 mm for winter; check the legal minimum locally); correct pressure, checked when cold, as pressure drops in the cold.
- Battery: test if older than 3–4 years; clean terminals.
- Coolant/antifreeze: correct mixture and level; have the freeze protection tested.
- Screen wash: winter mix rated for local temperatures.
- Wipers and lights: replace streaking blades; all bulbs and headlight lenses clean.
- Heating and demisting work; air-conditioning used briefly to dry the air.
- Door seals treated so they do not freeze; de-icer and scraper in the car.
- Electric cars: precondition while plugged in; expect reduced range in cold weather and plan charging stops with margin.

## Summer checklist
- Cooling system: coolant level, no leaks, radiator fan works.
- Air-conditioning cools well; service if it smells or blows weakly.
- Tyres: pressure for full load before long trips (see the door-frame sticker), no cracks or bulges, spare or repair kit ready.
- Oil level before a long journey; brakes checked if driving in mountains.
- Sun shade; never leave children or pets in a parked car, even briefly.

## Emergency kit
```
All year: warning triangle, high-visibility vest, first-aid kit, torch, phone charger,
          tyre inflator or spare and jack, jump leads or a jump starter, basic tools, water
Winter add: blanket, gloves, hat, snow brush and scraper, small shovel, chains (if needed),
          traction mats or sand, snacks
Summer add: extra drinking water, sun protection, coolant of the right type
```

## Before a long trip
Plan the route and stops, check the weather and road conditions, check tolls or vignettes needed, share the route with someone, and rest every couple of hours.

## Pitfalls
- Mixing tyre types on one axle or fitting only two winter tyres.
- Pouring hot water on a frozen windscreen; it can crack.
- Topping up with plain water in winter; it can freeze and damage the engine.
- Overloading the car or the roof box beyond the limits in the manual.
- Assuming one country's equipment rules apply abroad.

## Output
Hand back: a season-specific checklist marked owner/garage, the legal requirements to verify for each country, an emergency kit list, and a short pre-trip plan if a journey is planned.
