# Meeting notes and follow-ups

Meeting notes exist to record decisions and make sure agreed actions happen. Everything else is optional.

## Steps
1. **Gather the inputs.** Raw notes, attendees, date, purpose or agenda, and any documents shown. Ask who the follow-up goes to.
2. **Extract four things:**
   - **Decisions** - what was agreed, stated as a fact ("We will launch on 3 May").
   - **Action items** - verb + task + owner + due date. One owner per action, even if several help.
   - **Open questions / risks** - what was not resolved and who will find out.
   - **Key information** - only facts people will need later (numbers, constraints, dependencies).
3. **Fill the gaps honestly.** If an action has no owner or date, mark it `[owner?]` / `[date?]` and list it as a question; do not invent one.
4. **Keep it neutral.** Record outcomes, not who said what, unless attribution matters (for example a stakeholder's approval).
5. **Write the follow-up email**: thanks, decisions, action table, open questions, next meeting. Short enough to read on a phone.
6. **Check:** names and dates correct, every action has a verb, nothing confidential sent to the wrong audience, decisions match what people actually agreed (ask the user if unsure).

## Minutes template
```
Meeting: <title> - <date> - <duration>
Attendees: <names>   Absent: <names>
Purpose: <one line>

Decisions
1. ...

Action items
| Action | Owner | Due |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Send revised quote to the client | Nina | 14 Mar |
| Book venue for the workshop | [owner?] | [date?] |

Open questions
- ... (who will find out, by when)

Next meeting: <date/time or "none planned">
```

## Example follow-up email
```
Subject: Recap and actions - website launch sync, 10 Mar
Hi all, thanks for today. Summary below; please reply if I missed anything by Wed.
Decisions: launch moves to 3 May; budget stays as approved.
Actions: Nina - revised quote to the client by 14 Mar; Ivan - test plan by 17 Mar; venue booking - owner needed.
Open: do we need legal review of the new terms? (Maya to check by 12 Mar)
Next sync: 17 Mar, 10:00.
```

## Pitfalls
- Minutes that read like a transcript; nobody will read them.
- Actions owned by "team" or "we", which means nobody.
- Vague actions ("look into pricing"); make them concrete and checkable.
- Recording a discussion as a decision when it was only an idea.
- Sending notes days later; the follow-up is most useful the same day.

## Output
Hand back: the minutes (decisions, action table, open questions), the follow-up email ready to send, and a list of gaps the user must confirm (missing owners, dates or unclear decisions).
