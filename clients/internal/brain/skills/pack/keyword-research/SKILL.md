# Keyword research

Find the words real customers type, group them by intent and give every group one page. Be honest about data: without a keyword tool you can estimate demand only roughly.

## Steps
1. **Brief.** Ask for: what is sold or offered, to whom, which country and language, competitors the user knows, and the site's age and strength (new sites should aim at specific, low-competition phrases).
2. **Seed terms.** List 5-10 plain words a customer would use, not internal jargon: product names, problems solved, places.
3. **Expand** each seed with:
   - modifiers: best, cheap, near me, for beginners, vs, review, price, how to
   - questions: what, how, why, can, is (also check "People also ask" and autocomplete suggestions the user sees)
   - synonyms, spelling variants and the local language versions
4. **Label intent** for each phrase: informational (learn), commercial (compare), transactional (buy), navigational (brand).
5. **Estimate priority.** If the user has a tool (Search Console, a keyword planner or paid SEO tool), use its volume and difficulty. If not, rank by relevance to the business, purchase intent and how specific the phrase is; mark estimates as estimates.
6. **Cluster.** Group phrases that one page can answer together. One cluster = one page; the strongest phrase is its primary keyword.
7. **Map to pages.** Existing page that fits -> improve it; no page -> new page with suggested type and title.

## Template
| Cluster | Primary keyword | Variants | Intent | Priority | Page (existing / new) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner kits | watercolour set for beginners | best beginner watercolours, starter paint kit | commercial | High | New: comparison guide |
| Technique | how to blend watercolours | wet on wet technique | informational | Medium | New: tutorial |

## Example
Local bakery in Plovdiv. Seeds: sourdough bread, birthday cake, bakery.
- Transactional cluster: "birthday cake order Plovdiv", "custom cake Plovdiv" -> cake order page.
- Informational cluster: "how to store sourdough bread" -> short blog guide linking to the shop.

## Pitfalls
- High-volume generic words ("shoes") are usually unwinnable for a small site; prefer specific phrases.
- Do not create separate pages for near-identical phrases; it splits relevance.
- Volume numbers from different tools disagree; use them to compare, not as promises.
- Search behaviour is seasonal; note the season for gift, holiday and travel terms.

## Output
A clustered keyword table (as in the template), the top 5 opportunities with a one-line reason each, and the next three pages to create or improve.
