# Social post writing

Write for a scrolling thumb: the first line decides everything. One post, one idea, one action.

## Steps
1. **Brief.** Ask for: the message or source (article, news, product), platform(s), audience, goal (reach, comments, clicks, sales), brand voice, any must-include link, tag or disclosure.
2. **Find the angle.** A useful tip, a story, a surprising number, a strong opinion, a behind-the-scenes look or a question. Pick one.
3. **Write the hook** (first 1-2 lines, before "see more"): concrete, specific, no warm-up. Offer 3 options.
4. **Adapt to the platform:**
   - LinkedIn: short lines, a personal lesson or practical insight, 3-5 hashtags at most, link often better in the first comment if reach matters.
   - Instagram: caption supports the image or reel; first line is the hook, 3-10 relevant hashtags, link in bio or story.
   - Facebook: conversational, a question works well, link previews are fine.
   - X / Threads: one idea per post; for threads, the first post must stand alone.
   - TikTok / Shorts: write the spoken script with an on-screen hook in the first 2 seconds, plus a short caption.
5. **Add the call to action:** comment, save, share, click, reply - only one.
6. **Accessibility:** alt text for images, captions for video, camel case for multi-word hashtags (`#SmallBusiness`), emoji sparingly and not in place of words.
7. **Compliance:** mark paid partnerships and gifted products with the platform's disclosure tool; do not use others' photos, music or logos without rights.

## Example
Source: blog post "5 ways to cut cloud costs".
- LinkedIn hook: "We cut our cloud bill by 38% without touching a single feature. Here is what we switched off:" then 5 short lines, then "Which one would you try first?"
- X: "Your cloud bill has a 'forgot to turn it off' tax. 5 places to look:" plus a thread.
- Instagram carousel: slide 1 the hook, slides 2-6 one tip each, slide 7 "Save this for your next budget review".

## Checklist before posting
- The hook works on its own, without the image.
- One idea, one call to action.
- Facts, numbers and quotes are true and sourced.
- Tags and mentions are correct; people tagged agreed to it.
- Image or video has alt text or captions.
- Link works and has tracking parameters if the user measures clicks.
- Disclosure added for paid or gifted content.
- Spelling and names checked once more.

## Pitfalls
- Same text pasted to every network looks lazy and underperforms.
- Hashtag stuffing and engagement bait ("comment YES") can reduce reach.
- Character limits and algorithm preferences change; check the platform's current limits.
- Do not invent statistics or quotes to make a hook stronger.

## Output
For each platform: 3 hook options, the full post, hashtags, call to action, alt text or visual suggestion, and any disclosure needed.
