# School year checklist

Start early, turn every school letter into a dated task, and keep one place for all school paperwork.

## Steps
1. **Collect the facts.** Each child's age and grade, school or nursery, start date, any new school or first year, transport, after-school care, activities, special educational or health needs, and the official supply list if the school provided one.
2. **Build the before-term list** (3–4 weeks ahead) from the template below.
3. **Turn dates into calendar events**: term dates and holidays, parent meetings, trips, photo day, exam periods, payment deadlines. Ask the user to copy them from the official school calendar or letters.
4. **Set up a paperwork system**: one folder or tray for "to sign", one for "keep"; scan or photograph important letters.
5. **Plan routines**: shift bedtime gradually 1–2 weeks before start, practise the morning routine, do a trial run of the school route.
6. **Plan communication**: how the school sends messages (app, email, diary), contact details of class teacher, how to report absence.
7. **Add a term-by-term review**: what is working, upcoming deadlines, uniform sizes, supply refills.

## Before the first day
```
Documents and admin
[ ] Enrolment confirmed, required forms returned (medical, consent, photo permission)
[ ] Emergency contacts and who may collect the child given to the school
[ ] Health: check whether the school needs a medical note or vaccination record; tell school about allergies or medicines
[ ] Lunch / canteen registration and payment method
[ ] After-school care booked; transport or bus pass arranged
Supplies and clothing
[ ] Supply list items bought (check before buying extras)
[ ] Uniform or PE kit, labelled with name
[ ] Shoes that fit; indoor shoes if required
[ ] School bag sized to the child; water bottle, lunch box
Home
[ ] Homework spot with light and supplies
[ ] Morning and evening routine agreed
[ ] Device and screen-time rules for school nights
```

## During the year
- Weekly: check the school app or bag for letters, sign forms, update the family calendar.
- Monthly: check that supplies, lunch credit and clothing sizes are fine.
- Before exams or reports: plan revision time, talk about results calmly.
- End of year: return borrowed books, file reports, note what to change next year.

## Pitfalls
- Buying everything before seeing the official list; schools often specify brands or formats.
- Unlabelled clothing and bottles; they disappear.
- Missing deadlines hidden in letters; turn each one into a dated task the day it arrives.
- Changing sleep times only the night before school starts.
- Sharing children's personal data in public parent groups; keep private details in direct messages to the school.
- Ignoring signs of stress, bullying or learning difficulties; encourage talking with the class teacher, school counsellor or the child's doctor.

## Output
Hand back: a dated before-term checklist per child, the list of school dates to add to the calendar, a paperwork system suggestion, a routine adjustment plan, and a weekly/monthly checklist for the year.
