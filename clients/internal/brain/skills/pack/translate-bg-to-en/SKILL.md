# Translate Bulgarian to English

Aim for English a native reader would not notice as a translation. Shorter and more direct is usually better.

## Steps
1. **Clarify the brief.** Reader (client abroad, recruiter, authority, friend), variant (British or American English), formality and purpose. If it is a legal or official document, say a certified translator is needed for official use; offer a working translation.
2. **Read the whole text** and mark names, institutions, legal terms, abbreviations (ЕООД, ЕГН, НАП, ДДС) and anything ambiguous.
3. **Translate ideas, then trim.** Bulgarian tolerates long sentences and formal padding; split long sentences and remove filler („във връзка с гореизложеното“ → "therefore" or nothing).
4. **Handle Bulgarian-specific items.**
   - Names: transliterate by the official Bulgarian system (Щ → Sht, Ж → Zh, Ц → Ts, Ъ → A, Ю → Yu, Я → Ya), unless the person uses another spelling.
   - Institutions: give the English name, add the Bulgarian acronym once: National Revenue Agency (NRA/НАП).
   - Company forms: ЕООД → single-member limited liability company (EOOD), ООД → limited liability company (OOD); keep the legal form in the name.
   - Personal ID numbers (ЕГН): translate as "personal identification number"; never invent an equivalent like SSN.
5. **Apply English conventions** (checklist below).
6. **Read the English alone** for flow, articles (a/an/the) and tense consistency.
7. **List doubts** and terms with no exact English equivalent.

## English conventions checklist
- Decimal point and comma for thousands: 1,234.56.
- Dates: write the month as a word to avoid 03/04 confusion (3 April 2026 or April 3, 2026).
- Quotes "like this" (US) or 'like this' (UK); be consistent.
- Title case in headings is common in US English; sentence case is fine and modern.
- Currency before the amount: BGN 25, EUR 10, €10.
- Articles: Bulgarian has no indefinite article; add "a/an" where English needs it ("I am engineer" is wrong).
- Tense: Bulgarian present for planned actions often needs "will" or "going to" in English.

## False friends and traps
| Bulgarian | Wrong | Better |
|---|---|---|
| актуален | actual | current, up to date |
| евентуално | eventually | possibly, if needed |
| симпатичен | sympathetic | nice, likeable |
| аргумент | argument (quarrel) | point, reason |
| магазин | magazine | shop, store |
| фабрика / завод | fabric | factory, plant |
| конкурс | concourse | competition, tender |
| Уважаеми г-н Иванов | Respected Mr Ivanov | Dear Mr Ivanov |

## Example
Source: „Във връзка с Ваше запитване от 12.03.2026 г. Ви уведомяваме, че офертата е приложена към настоящото писмо.“
Literal: "In connection with your inquiry from 12.03.2026 we notify you that the offer is attached to the present letter."
Good: "Thank you for your enquiry of 12 March 2026. Please find our quotation attached."

## Pitfalls
- Over-formal openings and closings („С уважение“ → "Kind regards", not "With respect").
- Missing or wrong articles, and "informations", "advices", "equipments" (uncountable in English).
- Literal word order with the verb at the end of long clauses.
- Translating „лв.“ as "lv." in international texts; prefer BGN.
- Mixing British and American spelling (organise/organize, colour/color).

## Output
Return the English text in the source format, then a short note with the chosen variant (UK/US), transliterated names, terms without a direct equivalent and any open questions.
