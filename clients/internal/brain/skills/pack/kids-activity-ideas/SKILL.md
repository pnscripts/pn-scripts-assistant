# Kids activity ideas

Give a few well-matched ideas with clear instructions rather than a long generic list. Safety and supervision come first.

## Steps
1. **Collect the facts.** Ages, number of children, indoors or outdoors, time available, space, energy level wanted (calm or burn energy), materials at home, budget, any special needs or allergies, and whether an adult can join.
2. **Pick 3–5 ideas across types**: active, creative, building, pretend play, learning, helping at home.
3. **Write each idea briefly**: name, age range, time, materials, steps, how to make it easier or harder, cleanup.
4. **Add safety notes** where relevant: supervision, choking hazards, heat, water, tools, allergens.
5. **Offer a mini plan** if the child needs structure: a calm activity after an active one, a snack break, a clear ending.

## Ideas by type (adapt)
**Active (indoor)**
- Obstacle course from cushions, chairs and a blanket tunnel; time each round (3+).
- Freeze dance or "animal walk" relay (2+).
**Active (outdoor)**
- Nature scavenger hunt: list of things to find (a round stone, three kinds of leaves, something yellow) (4+).
- Chalk games: hopscotch, target throwing with beanbags (4+).
**Creative**
- Recycled-material building: boxes, tubes, tape; build a city or a marble run (5+).
- Story stones: draw pictures on stones or paper circles, draw three, invent a story (4+).
**Calm**
- Sorting games with buttons or pasta by colour and size (3+, supervise for choking under 3).
- Audio story with drawing what you hear (5+).
**Learning**
- Kitchen science: dissolving sugar vs sand, floating and sinking predictions (5+).
- Shop role-play with price tags to practise counting money (6+).
**Car or waiting room**
- "I spy", the alphabet game with signs, 20 questions, a travel bingo card (5+).

## Example idea card
```
Marble run on the wall | ages 6-10 | 45-60 min
Materials: cardboard tubes, painter's tape, marbles or ping-pong balls, a box at the bottom
Steps: 1) tape tubes at angles on a door or wall 2) test with one marble 3) adjust gaps
Easier: fewer tubes, wider ball. Harder: add a junction or a timer challenge.
Safety: marbles are a choking hazard for under-3s; use painter's tape so paint is not damaged.
Cleanup: remove tape slowly; keep tubes in a box for next time.
```

## Pitfalls
- Ideas above or below the child's age; it causes frustration or boredom.
- Small parts, button batteries, magnets or balloons near toddlers; these are serious hazards.
- Cooking or craft activities with allergens or hot tools without adult help.
- Plans requiring purchases when the user asked for things at home.
- Over-scheduling; free unstructured play is valuable too.

## Output
Hand back: 3–5 matched idea cards (age, time, materials, steps, easier/harder, safety, cleanup), and if asked a short schedule for the day or party.
