# Bulgarian invoice basics

Give general orientation on Bulgarian invoices. Rules come from the VAT Act (ЗДДС) and the Accountancy Act and they change: tell the user to verify current requirements on nra.bg and to consult an accountant before relying on them.

## Steps
1. **Establish the situation.** Ask: is the issuer VAT-registered? Is the buyer a business (with ЕИК / VAT number) or a consumer? Goods or services? Domestic, EU or outside the EU? Is this a first invoice, a correction or an advance payment?
2. **Pick the document type.**
   - Invoice (фактура) for a supply or an advance payment received.
   - Credit note (кредитно известие) to reduce an invoiced amount; debit note (дебитно известие) to increase it. Both reference the original invoice number and date.
   - Proforma (проформа фактура) is only an offer or payment request; it is not a tax document and does not replace the invoice.
3. **Check the mandatory fields** (see checklist). Missing fields are the most common reason an invoice is rejected by the buyer's accountant.
4. **Check numbering.** One continuous sequence of 10 digits, no gaps or duplicates, Arabic numerals only (e.g. `0000000001`). Several sequences are allowed only if set up deliberately; ask the accountant.
5. **Check timing.** A VAT-registered issuer generally must issue the invoice within a few days of the taxable event (the date of supply or of the advance payment); confirm the exact period in the current ЗДДС text.
6. **Check VAT treatment.** Rate shown and amount calculated per line or total; if no VAT is charged, the legal basis must be written (e.g. the article for reverse charge on intra-EU services, or a statement that the issuer is not VAT-registered).
7. **Check currency and rounding.** Use the currency required by current Bulgarian rules (verify on nra.bg; the country moved to the euro, so older templates may be wrong). Round VAT to two decimals.
8. **Corrections.** Never edit or delete an issued invoice. Issue a credit or debit note, or, for errors found before the buyer records it, follow the cancellation procedure your accountant uses and keep all copies.

## Checklist of fields
- [ ] Word "Фактура" (or the note type), number and date of issue
- [ ] Supplier: name, address, ЕИК, VAT number if registered
- [ ] Recipient: name, address, ЕИК / VAT number (for a business)
- [ ] Description and quantity of goods or kind and scope of services
- [ ] Date of the taxable event if different from the issue date
- [ ] Unit price without VAT, tax base, VAT rate, VAT amount, total
- [ ] Basis for not charging VAT, if applicable
- [ ] Payment method and bank details (practical, usually expected)

## Example: service to an EU business (reverse charge)
```
Фактура № 0000000042 / дата: 2026-03-04
Доставчик: Example Studio ЕООД, ЕИК 000000000, ДДС № BG000000000
Получател: Example GmbH, DE000000000
Услуга: разработка на уебсайт, 1 бр., 1 000.00
Данъчна основа: 1 000.00   ДДС: 0.00   Общо: 1 000.00
Основание: (cite the current ЗДДС article for reverse charge) / Reverse charge
```
Remind the user that intra-EU services usually also go into a VIES declaration; the accountant confirms.

## Pitfalls
- Issuing an invoice from a non-registered person with VAT charged on it.
- Gaps in numbering after deleting a draft in invoicing software.
- Treating a proforma as an invoice for accounting.
- Wrong buyer VAT number; check it in the EU VIES service before invoicing.
- Copying figures (thresholds, rates) from an old template instead of checking nra.bg.

## Output
Hand back: the document type to use, a filled or corrected invoice text, a list of missing or wrong fields, open questions for the accountant, and a reminder that this is general orientation and to consult an accountant.
