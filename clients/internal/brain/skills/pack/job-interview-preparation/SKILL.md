# Job interview preparation

Interviews reward preparation: knowing the role, having 5-6 ready stories with results, and asking good questions.

## Steps
1. **Collect:** job ad, CV, company name, interview format (phone, video, in person, panel, technical test), interviewers' roles if known, date and time.
2. **Research** (the user does this from official sources): what the company sells, customers, recent news, values, the team's role. Note 2-3 things that genuinely interest the user.
3. **Map the role:** list the top requirements; for each, pick one story from the user's experience as proof.
4. **Build 5-6 STAR stories** (Situation, Task, Action, Result), each 1-2 minutes spoken: a success, a failure and lesson, a conflict, leadership or initiative, a tight deadline, a customer or stakeholder moment. End each with a number or clear effect.
5. **Prepare core answers:**
   - "Tell me about yourself": present (role, strength), past (relevant highlights), future (why this job) - about 90 seconds.
   - "Why us / why this role": specific to the research.
   - "Weakness": a real, non-critical one, with what you are doing about it.
   - "Why are you leaving": positive, forward-looking, no criticism of the employer.
   - Salary expectations: see the salary negotiation skill; give a researched range or ask about the budget.
6. **Prepare 3-5 questions to ask:** what success looks like in 6 months, team challenges, how performance is measured, next steps in the process.
7. **Mock interview:** ask the user one question at a time, wait for their answer, then give specific feedback (structure, length, evidence, filler words) and a stronger version.
8. **Logistics:** route or tech check (camera, sound, light, quiet room), copy of CV, notes, clothing that fits the company's style, arrive 10 minutes early.
9. **After:** short thank-you email within a day; note questions asked for future interviews.

## STAR example
- S: "Our online shop's checkout errors doubled after a platform update."
- T: "As the support lead I had to stop the complaints and find the cause."
- A: "I grouped 120 tickets by error type, found one payment method failing, and worked with the developer on a fix and a customer message."
- R: "Errors went back to normal in two days, and we refunded affected customers within a week; complaints dropped by 80%."

## Common questions to practise
- Walk me through your CV.
- Describe a time you disagreed with a colleague or manager.
- Tell me about a mistake and what you learned.
- How do you handle several urgent tasks at once?
- Where do you see yourself in a few years?

## Pitfalls
- Memorising scripts word for word; prepare key points, not sentences.
- Answers without results or examples.
- No questions for the interviewer; it signals low interest.
- Lying about skills or experience.

## Output
A one-page prep sheet: company notes, requirement-to-story map, 5-6 STAR stories, core answers, questions to ask, logistics checklist - and, if requested, a mock interview with feedback.
