# Vocabulary builder

Words are learned in chunks and contexts, revisited over time, and actively used. A list of isolated translations is the weakest form.

## Steps
1. **Clarify the goal.** Language, level, purpose (travel, work field, exam, reading novels), how many words per week, and native language for translations.
2. **Choose words by usefulness.** Prioritise high-frequency words and those the learner will meet in their situation. For a text the user pastes, pick the words that block understanding, not every rare one.
3. **Present each word properly.**
   - word with the needed grammar (gender/article, plural, verb forms, irregular forms)
   - translation or short definition
   - one or two collocations ("make a decision", not "do a decision")
   - an example sentence relevant to the learner's life
   - notes: register (formal, informal, slang), false friends, pronunciation tip if tricky
4. **Group meaningfully.** By situation (at the doctor), by word family (decide, decision, decisive), or opposites; avoid teaching very similar words together on day one (left/right) as they get mixed up.
5. **Make recall material.** Cards with the native word or a gapped sentence on the front, target word on the back. Include reverse cards for words the learner must produce.
6. **Plan review.** Introduce 5–15 new words a day depending on time; review after 1 day, 3 days, a week and a month (or use an app's spaced-repetition scheduling).
7. **Make them active.** Give a task: write 5 sentences, tell a short story using 6 of the words, use them in the next conversation practice.

## Example entry (German, B1, work)
```
die Besprechung (-en)  - meeting
  collocations: eine Besprechung haben / leiten / verschieben
  example: Die Besprechung wurde auf Donnerstag verschoben.
  note: "das Meeting" is also common in offices; "die Sitzung" is more formal.
```

## Card format (tab-separated, for import)
```
the meeting (work)	die Besprechung, -en
Die ___ wurde auf Donnerstag verschoben.	Besprechung
```

## Pitfalls
- Long lists with one translation each and no context.
- Too many new words a day; reviews pile up and the learner quits.
- Ignoring gender, plural or irregular forms; they must be learned with the word.
- False friends left unmarked (for example English "eventually" is not "eventuell" in German).
- Rare or outdated words for a beginner; check that the words are current and common.

## Output
Hand back: a themed word table (word with grammar, meaning, collocation, example, note), a ready-to-import card list, a short review schedule, and one active-use task.
