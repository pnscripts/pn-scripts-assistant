# Home cleaning routine

Small daily resets keep a home manageable; deep cleaning is then a rotation, not a weekend crisis.

## Steps
1. **Collect the facts.** Home size and rooms, who lives there (children, pets, allergies), time available per day and per week, the worst problem areas, and who can share the work.
2. **Set a daily reset (10–20 min).** Dishes done or in the dishwasher, kitchen counters and hob wiped, quick tidy of the main room, laundry moved on, rubbish out when full.
3. **Make a weekly rotation.** One zone per day keeps each session short:
   - Mon: bathroom (toilet, sink, shower, mirror, floor)
   - Tue: kitchen (fronts, sink, microwave, floor)
   - Wed: dusting and surfaces, change towels
   - Thu: vacuum or mop all floors
   - Fri: bedrooms, change bed linen (every 1–2 weeks)
   - Weekend: laundry catch-up, fridge check before shopping
4. **Add a monthly list**: inside microwave and oven door, fridge shelves, skirting boards, light switches and door handles, washing machine seal and detergent drawer, dishwasher filter, extractor hood filter.
5. **Add a seasonal list**: windows, curtains, under furniture, mattress vacuum and turn, wardrobe sort, gutters or balcony, descaling the kettle and shower head.
6. **Share it.** Assign zones to people, rotate monthly, and give children age-suited tasks.
7. **Work top to bottom, dry to wet**: dust first, then wipe, then vacuum, then mop.

## Product safety
- Never mix bleach with ammonia, vinegar or other acids, or other cleaners; toxic gases can form.
- Ventilate while cleaning; wear gloves for strong products.
- Test new products on a hidden spot first; natural stone (marble) is damaged by acids such as vinegar and lemon.
- Keep products in original labelled containers, out of reach of children and pets.
- Follow the label for dilution and contact time; more product does not clean better.

## Quick method guide
| Problem | Approach |
|---|---|
| Limescale on taps | Descaler or diluted vinegar on a cloth (not on stone), rinse |
| Greasy hob | Warm water with washing-up liquid, soak, then non-scratch sponge |
| Mould spots on silicone | Mould remover per label with ventilation; persistent mould needs damp control |
| Smelly washing machine | Empty hot cycle with cleaner, clean seal and drawer, leave door open |

## Pitfalls
- A plan that needs two free hours a day; it will be dropped.
- Cleaning everything every week; rotate instead.
- Scrubbing delicate surfaces (glass hobs, stainless steel, wood) with abrasive pads.
- Treating recurring mould only by wiping; it often signals humidity or ventilation problems.

## Output
Hand back: a daily reset checklist, a weekly rotation table, monthly and seasonal lists, a chore split if several people share the home, and the safety notes relevant to the products they use.
