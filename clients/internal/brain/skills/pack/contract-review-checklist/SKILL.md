# Contract review checklist

Help the user understand a contract and prepare questions before signing. This is not legal advice. Recommend a lawyer when the contract is high-value, long-term, involves property, employment disputes, personal guarantees, exclusivity, non-competes, or when anything in it is unclear to the user after the review.

## Steps
1. **Context first.** Who are the parties, what is the deal, which side is the user on, value and duration, is it the other side's standard form or negotiable, deadline to sign.
2. **Read the whole document,** including annexes, schedules and anything "incorporated by reference". Note missing annexes.
3. **Go through the checklist** section by section; for each item record: clause number, what it says in plain words, risk (low / medium / high), suggested question or change.
4. **Check consistency:** names, dates, amounts and defined terms match across the text and annexes.
5. **Spot what is missing** (no termination clause, no payment deadline, no acceptance procedure).
6. **Draft redlines** as polite, specific proposals the user can send.
7. **Decide the escalation:** list the points a lawyer should look at.

## Checklist
- [ ] Parties: exact legal names, registration numbers, addresses, who signs and with what authority
- [ ] Scope: deliverables, quality standard, acceptance, what is excluded
- [ ] Price and payment: amount, currency, taxes, invoicing, due dates, late interest, expenses
- [ ] Timeline: start, milestones, delays and their consequences
- [ ] Changes: how scope or price can change, in writing
- [ ] Liability: caps, exclusions, penalties, indemnities, insurance
- [ ] Intellectual property: who owns results, licence back, pre-existing materials
- [ ] Confidentiality and personal data (processor agreement if relevant)
- [ ] Term, renewal, termination for convenience and for breach, notice periods
- [ ] Non-compete, non-solicitation, exclusivity: scope, territory, duration
- [ ] Governing law, courts or arbitration, language that prevails
- [ ] Signatures, dates, annexes listed and attached

## Example redline
> Clause 8.2: "The Contractor is liable for all damages." Proposal: "The Contractor's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim, except for wilful misconduct or gross negligence." Reason: unlimited liability is out of proportion to the contract value.

## Pitfalls
- Signing on the promise "we never enforce that clause".
- Oral side agreements not written into the contract.
- Auto-renewal with a long notice period hidden at the end.
- Accepting penalties for delay while the other side has no deadlines.
- Treating this checklist as a replacement for a lawyer on a high-stakes contract.

## Template: message with questions to the other party
```
Hello, thank you for the draft. Before signing we would like to clarify:
1. Clause 8.2 - could liability be capped at the fees of the last 12 months?
2. Clause 5 - what is the acceptance procedure and deadline?
3. Annex 2 is referenced but not attached; could you send it?
Kind regards, [Name]
```

## Output
Hand back: a 5-line summary of the deal, the filled checklist table with clause numbers and risk levels, missing items, proposed redlines, questions for the other party, points for a lawyer, and the statement that this is not legal advice.
