# Used car inspection

Never buy on looks alone. Check the papers, the history and the car itself, and get an independent inspection before any money changes hands.

## Steps
1. **Screen the advert.** Price far below market, seller abroad, "agent" or escrow service, pressure to pay a deposit before seeing the car, copied photos, or refusal to give the VIN: treat as a likely scam.
2. **Ask before travelling.** VIN, registration number, service history, number of owners, why it is sold, known faults, accident or repaint history, when the timing belt was changed (if fitted).
3. **Check the history** with official or reputable sources: registration and inspection records, outstanding finance or liens, theft and write-off records, mileage records where available. Tell the user which official sources to use in their country.
4. **Check documents at the viewing.** Seller's ID matches the registration document; VIN on the documents matches the car (windscreen, door pillar, stamped plate); service receipts and inspection certificates match the mileage.
5. **Walk-around (daylight, dry, car parked on level ground).**
   - Panel gaps uneven, paint shade differences, overspray on rubber: signs of repair.
   - Rust on sills, wheel arches, subframe, door bottoms.
   - Tyres: matching brands, even wear (uneven wear can mean alignment or suspension problems), age code on the sidewall.
   - Glass chips and cracks, lights intact.
6. **Under the bonnet (engine cold).** Ask to see a cold start; check oil (milky residue under the cap can signal a head gasket problem), coolant level and colour, leaks, belts, battery date.
7. **Interior.** Wear on the steering wheel, pedals and seat consistent with the mileage; all electrics, windows, air-conditioning, infotainment; damp carpets or a musty smell (leaks or flood damage); all warning lights come on with ignition and go out after start.
8. **Test drive (at least 20 minutes, mixed roads).** Smooth start, no smoke, straight-line stability, brakes without pulling or vibration, clutch and gearbox smooth, no knocks over bumps, steering without play.
9. **Independent inspection.** Have a mechanic or inspection service of the buyer's choosing check it, including a diagnostic scan.
10. **Pay safely.** Sign a written contract (seller and buyer data, VIN, mileage, price, known faults), pay by traceable bank transfer at the moment of handover, collect all keys and documents, and complete the registration transfer as required locally.

## Quick scorecard
```
Documents match        [ ]   History clean        [ ]   Service proof   [ ]
Body / rust            ok / minor / serious
Engine cold start      ok / noise / smoke
Test drive             ok / issues: ______
Mechanic inspection    done / pending
Decision: buy / negotiate (list faults and repair quotes) / walk away
```

## Pitfalls
- Seeing the car at night, in rain or with a warm engine already running.
- Paying a deposit online for a car you have not seen.
- Trusting the seller's own mechanic or a dashboard without a scan.
- Skipping the written contract with a private seller.
- Being rushed by "another buyer is coming"; walk away if pressured.

## Output
Hand back: red flags found so far, a tailored viewing checklist, questions for the seller, a negotiation list (faults and estimated repair impact), and a clear buy/negotiate/walk-away recommendation with reasons.
