# Clarity and tone rewrite

The goal is a text the intended reader understands on first read and receives the way the author means it. Facts, commitments and numbers do not change.

## Steps
1. **Pin down three things** before rewriting (ask if unclear):
   - *Reader*: who, how well they know the author, their mood or stake.
   - *Purpose*: inform, ask, decline, apologise, persuade, set a boundary.
   - *Target tone*: pick 2-3 adjectives, e.g. "warm, direct, brief" or "formal, firm, neutral".
2. **Extract the core message** in one sentence. If you cannot, the original is unclear; ask what the main point is.
3. **Restructure for the reader.** Main point or request first, then reasons, then details, then the next step or deadline.
4. **Adjust the tone with concrete levers:**
   - *Friendlier*: greeting and thanks, "we" and "you", contractions, one warm line.
   - *More formal*: full forms, no slang or emoji, precise nouns, polite conditionals.
   - *Firmer*: state the decision plainly, remove "just", "maybe", "sorry to bother", give a clear deadline.
   - *More diplomatic*: describe the issue rather than the person, acknowledge the other side, offer an option.
   - *Shorter*: delete throat-clearing openers, repeated points and hedges; one idea per sentence.
5. **Check for hidden tone problems:** sarcasm, all caps, many exclamation marks, "as I said before", "per my last email", blame words ("you failed to").
6. **Offer variants** when the right tone is a judgement call (for example a softer and a firmer version).

## Checklist
- [ ] Core message and every fact, date and amount preserved.
- [ ] Ask or decision is visible in the first two sentences.
- [ ] No passive-aggressive phrases or unexplained jargon.
- [ ] Length fits the channel (chat < email < report).
- [ ] Reads naturally aloud.

## Example
Original: "As I already said, the files were supposed to be here on Monday. I don't understand why this keeps happening. Please send them ASAP."
Rewrite (firm, polite): "Hi Maria, the files we agreed for Monday have not arrived yet. Could you send them by 12:00 tomorrow? If something is blocking you, tell me and we can adjust the plan together."

## Pitfalls
- Softening so much that the request disappears.
- Adding promises, apologies or admissions of fault the author did not make.
- Changing the meaning of a refusal into a "maybe".
- Uniform corporate tone that erases the author's personality; keep their characteristic words when they fit.
- Translating idioms of politeness literally between cultures; check the reader's expectations.

## Output
Return the rewritten text ready to paste, then one or two lines on what changed and why (tone levers used). If you offer variants, label them ("softer", "firmer") and recommend one.
