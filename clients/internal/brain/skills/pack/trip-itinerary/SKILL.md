# Trip itinerary

A good itinerary groups places by area, leaves breathing room and makes the few time-critical bookings obvious.

## Steps
1. **Collect the facts.** Destination(s), dates and arrival/departure times, who travels (ages, mobility, interests), pace (relaxed, balanced, packed), budget level, transport (car, public transport, walking), where they stay, and must-sees or must-avoids.
2. **Set the frame.** Arrival and departure days are half days at best. For multi-city trips, allow at least two nights per base; each change of base costs about half a day.
3. **List candidate activities** with typical visit length, opening days and whether booking ahead is usually needed. Tell the user to check current opening hours, prices and closures on official sites, as they change.
4. **Cluster by area.** Put sights that are close together on the same day; plan a loop or a line, not zig-zags across town.
5. **Shape each day.** One or two anchor activities, a lunch area, a lighter afternoon, an evening idea. Put the most popular sights early in the morning or at the end of the day to avoid crowds and heat.
6. **Add travel times** between stops with a realistic buffer (roughly +30 % in cities, more with children).
7. **Mark bookings** that sell out (timed museum entries, popular restaurants, trains, tours) and suggested booking timing.
8. **Add backups**: a rainy-day option, a free afternoon, an alternative if a place is closed.

## Template
```
Day 2 - Old town (walking)                    Book ahead
08:30  Cathedral (1 h) - go early              timed ticket
10:00  Walk the riverside to the market (20 min)
10:30  Market and coffee
12:30  Lunch near the main square
14:00  City museum (2 h)                       closed Mondays - check
16:30  Rest at the hotel
19:00  Sunset viewpoint (taxi 10 min), dinner nearby  reserve table
Rain plan: swap viewpoint for the covered food hall
```

## Pace guide
- Relaxed: 1–2 sights a day, long meals, free afternoons.
- Balanced: 2–3 sights, one rest break.
- Packed: 4+ sights; not recommended for more than 2–3 days in a row or with young children.

## Pitfalls
- Ignoring opening days; many museums close one weekday.
- Underestimating transfers, airport security and check-in/check-out times.
- Long drives on arrival day after a flight.
- Every hour scheduled; travellers get tired and plans collapse.
- Stating prices, hours or entry rules as certain; say "check the official site" for anything that changes.
- Safety and entry rules (visas, local laws, health advice) left out; point to the travel-documents checklist and official travel advice of the user's government.

## Output
Hand back: a short overview (route and bases), a day-by-day plan with times, areas and transport, a list of bookings to make with timing, rain or fallback options, and practical notes (local transport tips, areas to stay, what to check officially).
