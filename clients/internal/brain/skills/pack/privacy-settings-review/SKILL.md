# Privacy settings review

Help the user reduce what is shared, with whom, and for how long, without breaking what they rely on. Menus change often: describe what to look for and tell the user to check the platform's official help page if a setting has moved. Never ask for passwords.

## Steps
1. **Scope.** Which devices (Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS), browsers and accounts? What worries the user most: strangers seeing posts, ads and tracking, location, a specific app, an ex-partner or someone with past access?
2. **Run the safety check first if someone may have had access** to the device or account: signed-in devices, shared location, forwarding rules in email, recovery email and phone, unknown apps. Remove what the user does not recognise. If the user feels unsafe, point them to local support services and, in danger, 112.
3. **Phone permissions.** Open the permission manager and review by permission, not by app: location (set to "while using" or "never"), microphone, camera, contacts, photos, nearby devices, notifications. Remove unused apps.
4. **Location.** Turn off or auto-delete location history; check which apps have background location; turn off precise location where approximate is enough.
5. **Ads and tracking.** Turn off personalised ads in the platform account, reset or delete the advertising ID, limit app tracking requests, and in browsers block third-party cookies and enable tracking protection.
6. **Social media.** Set profile and past posts to friends or a chosen audience, hide phone number and email, turn off "find me by phone/email", review tagging approval, review apps connected through the account, check who can message you.
7. **Accounts and data.** Use the built-in privacy checkup or dashboard where offered; set auto-delete for activity history; review third-party app access; download your data to see what is stored; delete old accounts you no longer use.
8. **Smart devices.** Review voice recording storage, camera sharing, and household members.
9. **Record** what was changed and set a reminder for a review in 6 months.

## Checklist
- [ ] Unknown devices, sessions and forwarding rules removed
- [ ] App permissions reviewed by category; unused apps removed
- [ ] Location history off or auto-deleting; background location limited
- [ ] Personalised ads off; tracking protection on in browsers
- [ ] Social profiles: audience, discoverability, tagging, connected apps
- [ ] Activity auto-delete set; old accounts closed
- [ ] Review reminder set

## Example instruction
> On your phone open Settings, search for "Permission manager" (or "Privacy" then "Location Services"). Tap Location: for the flashlight app choose "Don't allow"; for maps choose "Allow only while using the app".

## Pitfalls
- Turning off settings that break safety features (e.g. location for emergency calls or find-my-device) without explaining the trade-off.
- Following "privacy tips" links from ads or pop-ups.
- Changing everything at once so the user cannot tell what broke an app.
- Assuming deleting an app deletes the account and its data.

## Quick wins in 10 minutes
1. Remove unknown signed-in devices from the main email account.
2. Set location to "while using" for every app that does not truly need it always.
3. Turn off personalised ads in the main platform account.
4. Make the social profile visible to friends only and hide the phone number.
5. Delete three apps not opened in the last month.

## Output
Hand back: a short platform-specific checklist in the order to follow, what each change does and its trade-off, the list of changes made, any items to verify on the platform's official help page, and the review reminder.
