# Content calendar

A calendar the user can keep beats an ambitious one they abandon in week two. Plan from capacity, not from wishes.

## Steps
1. **Collect inputs:** goals (awareness, leads, sales, community), channels, audience, available time per week, who creates and approves, existing material (articles, photos, reviews, FAQs).
2. **Set capacity.** Ask how many hours a week are realistic. Suggest a frequency that fits, e.g. 3 posts a week on one main channel rather than daily posts on five.
3. **Define 3-5 content pillars** tied to the goals, for example: educate (tips), prove (customer stories), show (behind the scenes), sell (offers), engage (questions, polls). Keep selling to roughly one post in five.
4. **Add key dates:** holidays and seasons relevant to the audience, product launches, events, industry days. Check local holidays for the user's country.
5. **Fill the calendar.** For each slot: date, channel, pillar, topic, format (post, carousel, short video, story, article, email), hook idea, call to action, owner, status.
6. **Plan reuse.** One larger piece (article, video, webinar) feeds several small posts across the month.
7. **Add a weekly 15-minute review**: what worked, what to repeat, what to drop.

## Template
| Date | Channel | Pillar | Topic | Format | Hook | CTA | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon 3 | Instagram | Educate | 3 mistakes when choosing running shoes | Carousel | "Your knees hate mistake #2" | Save | Ana | Draft |
| Wed 5 | LinkedIn | Prove | Customer story: club of 40 runners | Post + photo | "They ran 1,000 km in a month" | Comment | Ivo | Idea |
| Fri 7 | Email | Sell | Weekend fitting offer | Newsletter | "Free gait check this Saturday" | Book | Ana | Scheduled |

Status values: Idea, Draft, Ready, Scheduled, Published.

## Pitfalls
- Planning more than the team can produce; missed posts kill the habit.
- Only promotional posts; audiences tune out.
- Locking every slot; leave about 20% open for news and trends.
- Ignoring results; adjust the pillars monthly based on the analytics.

## Output
Pillars with a one-line purpose each, the calendar table for the requested period (in a pasteable format), the reuse plan and the weekly review questions.
