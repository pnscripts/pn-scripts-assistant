# Email newsletters and campaigns

Each email should have one goal, one main link and a reason for the reader to open it. Send only to people who agreed to receive it.

## Steps
1. **Define the campaign.** Goal (sale, sign-up, read, re-engage), audience segment, offer or news, send date, sender name.
2. **Check consent.** In the EU, marketing email needs prior opt-in consent (GDPR and e-privacy rules); existing customers may have narrower exceptions. Every email needs a visible one-click unsubscribe and the sender's identity and contact. Tell the user to check current rules and the mailing tool's requirements.
3. **Plan the sequence** if more than one email:
   - Welcome: 1 thank-you and what to expect, 2 best content or story, 3 first offer.
   - Launch: teaser, launch day, reminder with proof, last chance (only if there really is a deadline).
   - Re-engagement: "still interested?" with an easy keep/unsubscribe choice.
4. **Write the subject and preview text.** Specific benefit or curiosity, about 30-50 characters for the subject so it fits on phones; preview text continues it, never repeats it. Give 3 options for A/B testing.
5. **Write the body.** Personal greeting, one main point in the first two lines, short paragraphs, one clear button, plain-text fallback, sign-off from a real person or team.
6. **Check before sending:** links work, images have alt text, it reads well with images off and on a phone, the test email arrives, the segment is correct.
7. **Measure.** Clicks and conversions matter more than opens (privacy features of mail apps inflate open rates). Note unsubscribes and spam complaints; a rising rate means sending too often or off-topic.

## Template
```
Subject: Your spring garden plan is ready
Preview: 5 vegetables you can still sow this month
Hi {first_name},
[One-sentence hook]
[2-3 short paragraphs with the value]
[Button: Get the plan]
[Sign-off]
[Footer: company name, address, why you get this, unsubscribe]
```

## Sending rhythm
- Pick a frequency the user can keep (e.g. every two weeks) and tell subscribers at sign-up.
- Send at a consistent day and time; test a different slot only one variable at a time.
- Clean the list every few months: remove hard bounces and long-inactive contacts after a re-engagement attempt.
- Keep a simple log: date, segment, subject, clicks, sales, unsubscribes.

## Pitfalls
- Buying or scraping lists breaks consent rules and ruins deliverability.
- Misleading subjects ("Re:", "Your order") erode trust and can break the law.
- Too many links and big image-only emails land in spam more often.
- Sending everything to everyone; segment by interest or past purchases.

## Output
The campaign plan (goal, segment, dates), each email with 3 subject options, preview text, body and call to action, a pre-send checklist and the metrics to watch.
