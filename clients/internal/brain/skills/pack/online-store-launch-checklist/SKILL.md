# Online store launch checklist

Launch when a stranger can find a product, pay, receive it and return it without contacting you. Test as a real customer, end to end.

## Steps
1. **Collect context:** platform, countries sold to, product types (physical, digital, services), payment and shipping providers, VAT/tax registration status, launch date.
2. **Go through the checklist** and mark each item Done / Missing / Not applicable, with an owner.
3. **Place real test orders** (paid with a real card, then refunded) on desktop and phone, as a guest and with an account, with a discount code and to at least two shipping zones.
4. **Decide go / no-go.** Blockers: anything that stops payment, delivery, legal compliance or data protection. Everything else can follow in the first two weeks.

## Checklist
**Legal and trust (check local consumer law; in the EU distance-selling rules apply)**
- Company details: legal name, address, registration and VAT number, contact email and phone.
- Terms of sale, privacy policy, cookie notice with real consent, returns and withdrawal policy, delivery terms.
- Prices shown with taxes included for consumers where required; total cost visible before payment.
- Online dispute resolution or complaint information if required in the user's country.

**Products**
- Titles, descriptions, images, variants, stock levels, weights for shipping.
- Prices and compare-at prices correct; no test products left visible.

**Payments and tax**
- Live mode on (not test keys), 3-D Secure working, payout account verified.
- Tax settings per country; invoice or receipt generation checked with an accountant.

**Shipping**
- Zones, rates, free-shipping threshold, delivery times, packaging, label printing tested.

**Checkout and emails**
- Order confirmation, shipping and refund emails branded and correct; sender domain authenticated (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
- Abandoned cart email (only with proper consent rules).

**Technical**
- Custom domain with HTTPS, redirects from old URLs, 404 page, mobile layout, page speed tested.
- Analytics and conversion tracking respecting cookie consent; Search Console and sitemap submitted.
- Admin accounts with strong passwords and 2FA; backups enabled.

**Operations**
- Who answers customer messages and within what time; FAQ page; stock and fulfilment process.

## Pitfalls
- Testing only the happy path; also test a failed payment, an out-of-stock item and a refund.
- Copying another shop's legal pages; they must match the user's real processes.
- Forgetting tax registration thresholds when selling to other EU countries; check current rules and ask an accountant.

## Output
A table of all items with status and owner, the list of launch blockers, the test-order results, and a 2-week post-launch to-do list.
