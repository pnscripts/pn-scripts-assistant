# Bookkeeping basics

Help the user keep clean, complete records so their accountant can file correctly. You organise and check; the accountant decides tax treatment. Say so when a question goes beyond record-keeping.

## Steps
1. **Separate money.** Recommend a separate bank account for the business. Mixed personal and business transactions are the most common source of errors.
2. **Set up the ledger.** A spreadsheet with one row per transaction:
   `Date | Document no. | Counterparty | Description | Category | Net | VAT | Total | Paid from/to | Document file`
3. **Agree categories** with the accountant once. A starting set: Sales, Other income, Materials, Software and subscriptions, Equipment, Rent, Utilities, Travel, Marketing, Fees and bank charges, Taxes and contributions, Owner withdrawals.
4. **Capture every document.** Each row has a document (invoice, receipt, contract, bank fee notice). Name files consistently, e.g. `2026-03-04_supplier_invoice-0042.pdf`, and keep them in one folder per month.
5. **Categorise.** For a pasted CSV, propose a category per row, mark unclear rows with "?" and ask; never guess the business purpose of a personal-looking expense.
6. **Reconcile monthly.** The ledger's closing bank balance must equal the bank statement. Investigate every difference: missing fee, duplicate import, transfer between own accounts.
7. **Track open items.** Unpaid sales invoices (receivables) and unpaid bills (payables) with due dates.
8. **Monthly close.** Ledger complete, bank reconciled, documents attached, open items reviewed, package sent to the accountant by the agreed day.

## Monthly close checklist
- [ ] All bank and card transactions imported
- [ ] Every row has a category and a document
- [ ] Bank balance matches the statement
- [ ] Receivables and payables list updated
- [ ] Cash receipts entered
- [ ] Questions for the accountant listed
- [ ] Backup of the folder made

## Example
Bank line: `2026-03-12  -24.99  CLOUDSTORAGE*SUB  card 1234`
Ledger row: `2026-03-12 | INV-88213 | Cloud storage provider | Monthly storage plan | Software and subscriptions | 24.99 | 0.00 | 24.99 | business card | 2026-03-12_cloudstorage_INV-88213.pdf`
Note: foreign service, no Bulgarian VAT on the invoice; flag for the accountant (may need reverse-charge treatment).

## Pitfalls
- Paying business costs from a personal card without recording it.
- Deleting or overwriting rows instead of correcting with a new entry.
- Keeping only bank lines without the underlying invoices.
- Treating owner withdrawals as expenses.
- Leaving reconciliation until year end, when nobody remembers the details.

## Keeping records safe
- Keep documents for the period required by law; ask the accountant for the current period, which is often many years.
- Scan or photograph paper receipts soon; thermal paper fades.
- Back up the ledger and the document folder every month to a second place.
- After the month is closed, keep a read-only copy so past figures do not change by accident.
- Record who has access to the folder and the bank account.

## Output
Hand back: the ledger structure or the categorised rows, the list of unclear items and missing documents, the reconciliation result with any differences explained, and questions for the accountant. Remind the user that tax treatment is the accountant's call.
