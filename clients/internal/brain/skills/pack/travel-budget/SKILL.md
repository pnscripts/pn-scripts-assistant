# Travel budget

A trip budget is a set of estimates with ranges, checked against real quotes, plus a buffer. Prices change constantly, so every number must be verified at booking time.

## Steps
1. **Collect the facts.** Destination, dates and number of nights, travellers, travel style (budget, mid-range, comfort), how they get there, where they want to stay, planned activities, home currency, and total budget if fixed.
2. **List the categories.**
   - Getting there and back: flights, trains, fuel and tolls, parking, airport transfers, luggage fees.
   - Accommodation: per night × nights, plus city or tourist taxes and cleaning fees.
   - Food and drink: per person per day (breakfast, lunch, dinner, coffee, snacks).
   - Local transport: passes, taxis, car rental, fuel.
   - Activities: tickets, tours, equipment hire.
   - Fixed extras: travel insurance, visas or entry fees, roaming or eSIM, vaccinations if required.
   - Shopping and souvenirs.
   - Buffer: 10–15 % of the total for surprises.
3. **Estimate with real data.** Ask the user to check current prices on official or booking sites for big items; give daily food and transport as a low–high range and say they are rough.
4. **Compute totals**: per category, per person, per day and for the trip, in home currency, noting the exchange rate and date used.
5. **Compare with the budget.** If over, suggest levers in order of impact: dates (off-season, midweek flights), accommodation type and location, fewer bases, self-catering some meals, city passes, free activities.
6. **Plan payments.** Card fees abroad, cash needs, deposits and cancellation terms.
7. **Track during the trip** with a simple daily log.

## Template
```
Trip: Lisbon, 5 nights, 2 adults, mid-range           Rate used: 1 EUR = home currency (date)
Category            Basis                         Low     High
Flights             2 × return incl. bag          ___     ___
Accommodation       5 nights + tourist tax        ___     ___
Food                2 × 5 days × daily range      ___     ___
Local transport     transport cards + 2 taxis     ___     ___
Activities          3 tickets + 1 day trip        ___     ___
Insurance & data    policy + eSIM                 ___     ___
Subtotal                                          ___     ___
Buffer 10-15 %                                    ___     ___
TOTAL / per person / per day                      ___     ___
```

## Daily spending log
```
Date | Item | Category | Amount | Paid by | Currency | Note
```
For shared trips, record who paid and settle at the end: total ÷ people = fair share; each person's balance = paid − fair share.

## Pitfalls
- Forgetting airport transfers, luggage fees, tourist taxes and deposits.
- Dynamic currency conversion at card terminals and cash machines; usually choose to pay in local currency.
- Using last year's prices as facts; always label estimates and the date.
- No buffer; one taxi in the rain or a lost item breaks the budget.
- Advising how to fund the trip with debt; keep to budgeting and suggest saving ahead instead.

## Output
Hand back: a category table with low and high estimates, totals per person and per day, the comparison with the user's budget, the top savings options, what to verify before booking, and a tracking template.
