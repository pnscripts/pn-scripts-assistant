# Dashboard warning lights

Colour signals urgency. Red means stop safely as soon as possible; amber/yellow means have it checked soon; green, blue or white is information. The owner's manual has the exact meaning for each car.

## Steps
1. **Get the details.** Colour, shape (ask for a photo or a description), whether it is steady or flashing, any message text, when it appeared, and symptoms: noise, smell, smoke, loss of power, steering or braking feel, temperature gauge.
2. **Triage the urgency.**
   - Red or flashing, or any symptom affecting brakes, steering, temperature or oil pressure: tell the user to stop safely as soon as it is safe, switch off the engine, and not continue driving.
   - Amber steady with the car behaving normally: usually safe to drive carefully to a garage soon; avoid long trips.
3. **Identify the symbol** using the table and confirm with the owner's manual (index "warning lights").
4. **Give the immediate action** for that light.
5. **Say what to tell the mechanic**: the symbol, colour, when it appeared, conditions (cold start, motorway, after refuelling), and any stored fault codes if a scan is done.
6. **Safety first when stopping**: hazard lights, pull over away from traffic, passengers out behind a barrier if on a motorway, warning triangle and high-visibility vest if required locally, call roadside assistance or the emergency number (112 in the EU) if anyone is at risk.

## Common lights
| Symbol (typical) | Colour | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil can | Red | Low oil pressure | Stop now, engine off; check level when cool; do not drive if pressure warning stays |
| Thermometer in liquid | Red | Engine overheating | Stop, engine off, wait to cool; never open the hot coolant cap |
| Battery | Red | Charging fault | Turn off non-essentials, drive only a short distance to safety or garage; car may stop |
| Brake circle with "!" or "BRAKE" | Red | Parking brake on, low brake fluid or brake fault | Release handbrake; if still on, stop and get help |
| Person with seat belt | Red | Seat belt not fastened | Fasten belt |
| Engine outline | Amber steady | Engine or emissions fault | Book a diagnostic soon |
| Engine outline | Amber flashing | Severe misfire | Reduce speed, avoid load, get it checked immediately; can damage the catalytic converter |
| Horseshoe with "!" | Amber | Low tyre pressure | Check and inflate all tyres when cold; inspect for punctures |
| "ABS" | Amber | ABS fault; normal brakes usually work | Drive gently, get checked |
| Steering wheel with "!" | Red/amber | Power steering fault | Steering may be heavy; drive carefully to a stop or garage |
| Coil symbol | Amber | Diesel glow plugs; flashing = fault | Wait for it to go out before starting; if flashing, get checked |
| Filter with dots | Amber | Diesel particulate filter clogging | Follow manual (often a sustained drive at steady speed) or get help |
| Airbag person | Red/amber | Airbag system fault | Airbags may not deploy; get checked soon |

## Pitfalls
- Assuming meaning from another car's symbol set; shapes differ by brand, check the manual.
- Clearing codes with a scanner to make the light go away without fixing the cause.
- Continuing to drive with red oil or temperature warnings "to get home"; this can destroy the engine.
- Diagnosing a specific part from the light alone; a scan and inspection are needed.

## Output
Hand back: the likely meaning (with a reminder to confirm in the manual), urgency (stop now / check soon / information), the immediate step-by-step action, safety notes, and what to tell the mechanic.
