# Terms of use

На български: [условия за ползване](terms.bg.md).

Version 1, 13 November 2026

Please read these before you start. You accept them once; if they change, you
are asked again.

## It is an AI system

PN Scripts Assistant is an artificial intelligence (AI) system. What it says,
writes and does comes from a language model, not from a person. Its answers
can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, so check anything that matters
(health, money, law, safety) before you rely on it or send it.

## What stays on this computer

What it learns about you (conversations, memory, settings) is kept in its
folder on this computer or on the drive you chose, encrypted. It leaves the
computer only for what you use: with a hosted model, your message, the memory
recalled for it and your profile go to the provider you chose, under that
provider's terms; reading the web fetches the pages; mail goes through your
mail server. In private mode it uses only local models and does not read the
web. Once a day it asks GitHub whether a new version is out and sends nothing
about you; that can be switched off.

## It acts for you

Unless you change it in Settings, it acts without asking first, including when
it sends mail, installs programs and changes files. Every action is recorded
and can be looked at again. You are responsible for what it does on your
behalf and for what you send.

## What it sends is marked

Mail it sends carries the header AI-Disclosure: mode=ai-originated, and the
PDF and Word documents it writes say "AI-generated" in their properties, so
others can tell they were written with AI. The words themselves are not
changed.

## No warranty

It is free software under the MIT licence and comes as is, without warranty of
any kind, as that licence says.

## When the terms change

A new version of the program may come with new terms; then it asks you again
before you go on. You can read these terms again at any time on the "What it
runs on" page, and in the documentation (docs/guide/terms.md).


## Where you accept them

The first time PN Scripts Assistant opens (and again after an update that
changes these terms) it shows them before anything else, in English or
Bulgarian, with one button to accept. Until they are accepted it answers
nothing, listens to nothing and starts no work of its own; the acceptance is
kept beside its memory in `terms-accepted.json`, with the version accepted and
when. The same text stays readable afterwards on the "What it runs on" page,
under "Terms of use".

These terms are how the program tells you, once and clearly, that it is an AI
system, as article 50 of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) asks at the
latest at the first interaction. After that it does not keep repeating it; it
answers truthfully when you ask.
